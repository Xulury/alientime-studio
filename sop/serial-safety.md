# Serial safety

1. Serial numbers never enter the listing pipeline. They stay in the business's own records and are shown to
   serious buyers on request.
2. Every photo is scanned locally (OCR at four rotations) before anything else. Serial-like text, numbered
   markings and edition numbers get a **visible** redaction block — not a seamless retouch — so buyers can see
   something was hidden.
3. A second OCR pass must read no digits inside any redacted area. If it does, the frame is blocked.
4. OCR is not proof. Frames with findings, lots of engraved text, or no OCR available need a person to check
   them ("Checked") before they can go to any cloud AI service or be approved.
5. Casebacks, movements, warranty cards, papers and invoices are never sent to a cloud AI service, even after
   checking. Papers and invoices are never used in listings.
6. The recognised text itself is never stored or logged.
