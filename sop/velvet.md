# Studio backgrounds

- House look: deep green velvet. Locally generated by default (no AI, no cost). An AI velvet plate
  (OpenAI image model) can be chosen per photo or as the default.
- A model may only make the background. Every watch pixel comes from the photo; the fidelity gate checks the
  final image against the original inside the watch outline and blocks approval if they differ.
- Edited sets (already on a clean studio background) keep their background and are only re-cropped.
- Raw camera and phone/WhatsApp photos are cut out and placed on the house background with a soft contact shadow.
- Allowed corrections: white balance, exposure, contrast and warmth, applied identically to the whole watch.
  No retouching of dial, hands, logos, engravings, condition marks or accessories.
